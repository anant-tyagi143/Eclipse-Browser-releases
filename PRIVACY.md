Eclipse Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Eclipse stores your browsing history, settings, and theme on your own computer. Private tabs are never recorded.

Eclipse does not collect analytics, require an account, or send your personal data to the developer.

When you browse, websites and your chosen search engine receive requests the same way they would from any browser.

Eclipse downloads tracker-blocking lists from the internet and checks GitHub for updates. These requests do not include personal data.

You can clear your history at any time from the History page.
